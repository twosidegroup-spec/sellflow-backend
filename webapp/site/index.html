Payments, without the hand-waving

We tell you exactly what a payment status means

A notification appearing on a phone is evidence of a message — not proof that money arrived. SellFlow keeps every stage separate so you always know what you're looking at:

  • Notification received — an event reached your account (via the SellFlow bridge or manual entry).
  • Parsed — amount, transaction ID and sender were extracted and validated.
  • Matched to an order — the engine found a same-business order by amount, phone and timing.
  • Reconciled — the transaction is recorded against the order with an audit trail.
  • Needs review — nothing matched confidently. It waits for you; it is never auto-confirmed.

A typed transaction ID or a shared SMS is not independent proof of payment, and SellFlow never presents it as one. For merchant-channel payments, SellFlow's architecture supports signed provider webhooks where an official integration is in place.

Security & privacy

Controls that are actually implemented

Listed only because they exist in the product today — each item maps to code and automated tests.

Business-level isolation

Every request resolves your business from your credential; cross-business reads and writes are denied and covered by tests.

Hashed device credentials

Pairing codes and device tokens are stored only as HMAC-SHA256 digests. Plaintext is shown once and never logged.

Replay & duplicate protection

Payment events are unique per business, provider and transaction ID at the database level.

No SMS bodies stored

Only normalised payment fields are transmitted and stored — never the raw message text.

Fail-closed configuration

Production refuses to start with missing or placeholder secrets; a wildcard CORS origin cannot be combined with credentials.

Rate-limited authentication

Registration, sign-in, pairing and ingestion endpoints are rate-limited server-side.

Questions

Straight answers

How does payment detection actually work?

Two supported paths: an Android device paired to your business runs the SellFlow payment bridge, which parses recognised bKash-style payment messages and forwards only normalised fields (amount, transaction ID, sender) over an authenticated, rate-limited channel; or you record a payment manually from the order screen. Every event is deduplicated and matched to one of your orders — or placed in your review queue.

Does a “Reconciled” badge mean the provider confirmed the money?

No — and we're careful about the words. It means SellFlow received an event, parsed it, matched it to an order in your business and recorded it with a full audit trail. Only an authorised provider integration (for example signed merchant webhooks) can attest that a payment is provider-verified, and SellFlow's architecture is built to support that where such an integration exists.

Can SellFlow read SMS on an iPhone?

No. iOS does not expose the SMS inbox to apps, and the web platform cannot read arbitrary SMS on any phone. On iPhone you confirm payments manually; on Android the optional bridge app handles SMS detection.

Is my shop's data separate from other sellers?

Yes. Every API request resolves your business from your credentials, and all business-owned resources (orders, products, customers, payments, analytics, settings) are scoped to it. Cross-business access is denied and verified by automated tests in the codebase.

What happens to a payment that doesn't match any order?

It is stored as an unmatched event in your review queue — never guessed onto an order. You can point it at the right order manually, and duplicates or replays are blocked at the database level.
